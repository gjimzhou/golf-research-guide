# Hawaii — Kauai Golf

## Princeville Makai

Princeville Makai is one of Kauai's major scenic-golf targets.

For late-afternoon play, cart-return policies and realistic pace matter as much as nominal sunset time. A tee time that looks feasible on paper may not leave enough margin to complete the holes that matter in good light.

Weather disruption is also a material Kauai planning risk. Storm damage can affect trees, power, course conditioning, and reopening schedules even when hotels elsewhere on the island remain operational.

### Planning lesson

Treat course status as a first-class itinerary dependency. If golf materially affects the hotel or island choice, align cancellation deadlines with the date by which course conditions can reasonably be confirmed.

## Poipu Bay

Poipu Bay is a strong south-shore alternative and diversifies an itinerary geographically as well as aesthetically.

The important operational lesson is visible in its own current website: as of 2026-09-22, Poipu Bay lists the course as **closed September 21–22, 2026 for aeration**.

A traveler using only a generic ranking or “course open this season” description could easily miss that.

### Before paying premium destination pricing

Verify:

- aeration/punching dates;
- temporary greens;
- overseeding or recovery windows;
- storm-related closures;
- cart-path restrictions;
- renovation projects;
- recent condition reports.

A course can be technically open while still offering a materially compromised premium-golf experience.

## North shore vs. south shore

The golf choice should be integrated with where the rest of the trip is based.

A cross-island round can be worthwhile for a distinct course, but the real cost includes:

- drive time;
- traffic uncertainty;
- early departure;
- lost resort/beach time;
- club handling;
- weather differences between island regions.

## Source discipline

For maintenance and reopening questions, prefer:

1. the course's own website;
2. direct confirmation from the golf shop;
3. only then recent third-party reports.

Third-party reviews are useful for subjective course character but are a weak source for today's greens or closure status.
