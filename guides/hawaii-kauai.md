# Hawaii — Kauai Golf

## Princeville Makai

Princeville Makai is one of Kauai's major scenic-golf targets. For late-afternoon play, cart-return policies and realistic pace matter as much as nominal sunset time; a tee time that looks feasible on paper may not leave enough margin to complete 18 holes comfortably.

Weather disruptions are an important Kauai-specific planning risk. Storm damage can affect trees, power, course conditioning, and reopening schedules even when hotels elsewhere on the island remain operational.

### Planning lesson

Treat course status as a first-class itinerary dependency. If golf is a material reason for choosing a hotel or part of the island, align cancellation deadlines with the date by which course conditions can be confirmed.

## Poipu Bay

Poipu Bay is a strong south-shore alternative and can diversify an itinerary geographically as well as aesthetically.

Before paying premium destination-golf pricing, verify aeration/punching dates, temporary greens, overseeding or recovery windows, storm-related closures, cart-path restrictions, and current course-condition reports.

A course can be technically open while still offering a materially compromised premium-golf experience.
