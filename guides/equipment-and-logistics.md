# Equipment and Golf Logistics

## Airline and bag protection

Golf-club headcovers and iron covers are protective choices, not normally airline carriage requirements. Verify airline sports-equipment rules, case restrictions, weight limits, oversize fees, liability exclusions, and whether a stiff-arm or similar support is appropriate.

## Multi-hotel trips

Before travel, ask each hotel whether it can receive clubs before check-in, store clubs after check-out, transfer bags internally to golf operations, or arrange a courier to another property.

Do not assume neighboring luxury resorts have an inter-resort golf-bag transfer partnership.

## Rental car vs. shipping

A rental car is often simplest when hotels and courses are geographically aligned. Shipping becomes more attractive when it removes airport handling or when a trip includes several non-golf segments where carrying clubs is inconvenient.

## Pairing expectations

A request for a twosome should be treated as a request unless the course explicitly guarantees empty adjacent slots or sells the remaining positions. Destination courses often prioritize full utilization during busy periods.
