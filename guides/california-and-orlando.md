# California and Orlando Golf Notes

## California

For Napa, Half Moon Bay, and Big Sur itineraries, golf works best as a complement to the hotel and destination experience rather than as the organizing principle of the trip.

Useful selection criteria include scenery and sense of place, travel time from the hotel, difficulty relative to a vacation round, course-condition risk, and whether club shipping or storage reduces logistical friction.

A visually memorable but playable course can create more trip value than a more famous course that consumes an entire day or is unnecessarily punitive.

## Orlando

The Waldorf Astoria / Disney area provides a convenient cluster of resort-golf options. Disney Magnolia, Lake Buena Vista, and Palm are examples of courses that can be compared primarily on current condition, difficulty, routing, and schedule fit.

The general lesson is to optimize for trip flow rather than maximum round count.
